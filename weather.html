🌡️ Senate Passes Consumer Bill of Rights to Protect Alaskans from Crypto ATM Scams

Today, May 12, 2026, the Alaska State Senate passed Senate Bill 249, which establishes a Consumer Bill of Rights intended to protect Alaskans from scams involving crypto ATMs. SB 249 requires virtual currency kiosks (also known as Bitcoin ATMs) to display mandatory fraud warnings, acquire licenses, enact transactions limits, and cap transaction fees.
