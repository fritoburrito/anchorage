About AK Pulse Live

Home • Full Site • Channel View • RSS Feed

AK Pulse Live is a custom automated news and weather feed built to highlight Alaska-first stories, live headlines, weather updates, science news, and selected national and world coverage.

The site is powered by public RSS feeds, scheduled automation, and GitHub Pages. Headlines are collected automatically, sorted with local Alaska stories prioritized, and published into multiple views including a simple feed page, a full news-style homepage, and a channel-style display.

What This Site Includes

AK Pulse Live includes Alaska and Anchorage news, weather from the National Weather Service, national and world headlines, NASA/science updates, and a live-style channel view designed for quick scanning.

How It Works

The feed updates automatically on a schedule. New items are imported, categorized, sorted, and published as both a web page and an RSS feed. Local Alaska-related stories are given priority so they appear more prominently.
